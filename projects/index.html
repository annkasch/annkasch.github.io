Projects

Rare-event estimation, simulation-based inference, detector hardware and scientific computing at Berkeley Lab, and graduate projects in computer vision and robot learning. The theory behind the methods is written up under Methods & Perspectives.

01Rare events

  • Project · ICLR 2025 Spotlight

    RESuM: a rare-event surrogate model for detector design

    A conditional neural process and a multi-fidelity Gaussian process that found a LEGEND neutron moderator with 66.5% less background using 3.3% of the compute, now applied across neutrino, dark-matter and astrophysics experiments.
